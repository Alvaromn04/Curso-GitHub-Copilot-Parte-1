# Close & Retrospective Process

This phase converts delivery results into reusable learning.

## Objectives

- Evaluate outcomes against goals and metrics.
- Capture what worked, what failed, and why.
- Convert learnings into actionable improvements.

## Core Activities

1. Review delivery performance and stakeholder feedback.
2. Run retrospective with PM, PdM, Developers, and QA.
3. Create improvement actions with owners and due dates.

## Exit Criteria

- Final outcome summary documented.
- Retrospective action items logged and assigned.
- Project formally closed with follow-up plan agreed.
